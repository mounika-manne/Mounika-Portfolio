About

Grounded in data, focused on impact

I am an experienced researcher specializing in remote sensing, ecological modelling, and environmental science. My work spans geospatial analysis, time-series assessment of coastal ecosystems, and hydrological modelling for flood forecasting.

I'm drawn to problems where satellite observation meets ground reality — tracking shoreline change over decades, modelling how river basins respond to extreme rainfall, and translating raw pixels into decisions that protect people and places.

Research Focus

Four lenses on a changing environment

Each area below feeds into the others — satellite data grounds the models, and the models sharpen where the next observation should look.

01 · EARTH OBSERVATION

Remote Sensing & GIS

Multi-temporal satellite imagery analysis, land cover classification, and spatial modelling to track environmental change at scale.

02 · COASTS

Coastal Ecosystem Monitoring

Time-series assessment of mangroves, wetlands, and shoreline dynamics under climate and anthropogenic pressure.

03 · WATER

Hydrological Modelling

Flood forecasting and river basin modelling that combines rainfall data, terrain, and land use to predict inundation risk.

04 · SYSTEMS

Ecological Modelling

Simulating ecosystem response to environmental drivers to support conservation planning and land management decisions.
