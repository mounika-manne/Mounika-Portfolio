About

Grounded in data, focused on impact

I hold a Ph.D. in Remote Sensing and GIS from BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, along with an M.E. in Irrigation and Water Management and a B.Tech. in Civil Engineering. Over 10+ years, I've specialized in satellite-derived data processing and ecosystem dynamics - with particular depth in ecological modelling, investigating the relationships between vegetation indices and ecosystem productivity.

My doctoral research examined mangrove ecosystems across four objectives: spatial dynamics, phenological patterns, climate impacts, and productivity. I devised a new method for estimating mangrove phenology by combining climatic factors with satellite-derived vegetation indices, and modelled Gross Primary Productivity through path analysis, validated against eddy covariance flux tower data.

I currently work as a Consultant - Research Scientist (Remote Sensing & GIS) at Vassar Labs, in collaboration with SHAR ISRO and RTGS, Government of Andhra Pradesh - analyzing satellite and drone data for disaster monitoring, crop health assessment, and climate resilience, and building automated Python/R/MATLAB workflows for large-scale geospatial data.

Research Focus

Four lenses on a changing environment

Each area below feeds into the others — satellite data grounds the models, and the models sharpen where the next observation should look.

01 · EARTH OBSERVATION

Remote Sensing & GIS

Processing Sentinel, MODIS and drone data for land-use change, disaster monitoring and crop health assessment using ENVI, QGIS, ArcGIS, SNAP and Google Earth Engine.

02 · COASTS

Mangrove & Coastal Ecosystem Dynamics

Ph.D. research on mangrove spatial dynamics, phenology and climate impacts using SAR (Sentinel-1) and optical (Sentinel-2, Landsat) time series.

03 · WATER

Hydrological Modelling & Flood Analysis

Watershed delineation, hyetograph design and 1D/2D flood analysis for 50- and 100-year storms using PCSWMM and HEC-RAS.

04 · SYSTEMS

Ecological & Statistical Modelling

Path and harmonic analysis linking vegetation indices to Gross Primary Productivity, validated against eddy covariance flux tower data.
